System Guide

Everything you need to use your Smart Home System: what each card shows, what every button does, and how the system makes its decisions.

1Quick start

You can be up and running in three steps.

Open the dashboard

On the home page press the big Process button. The monitoring dashboard opens and starts updating by itself every couple of seconds. You never need to refresh the page.

Read the status

The Home Status banner at the top tells you everything at a glance: SECURE (all normal), ALERT (a breach is active) or SYSTEM OFF. The tiles below show each sensor.

Take control when you need to

Use the Homeowner Controls panel to switch lights, open or close the gate and doors, choose AC or Heater, or turn the whole system on or off. Every button works instantly, with no page reload.

💡Good to know: every time the system starts, everything is on automatic. Your manual choices only apply after you press a button, and Resume normal always hands control back to the system.

2Dashboard tour

What each part of the dashboard shows.

🛡️Home Status

The overall verdict: SECURE, ALERT or SYSTEM OFF. It turns red when the alarm is active.

🌡️House Temperature

The current temperature with a plain-word condition (Comfortable, Hot or Cold). Hot above 35°C, cold below 18°C.

🚪Gate & Door

SECURE / ALL CLOSED when quiet, BREACH when the sensors detect movement or an unexpected opening, OPEN when you opened it yourself.

🏠Occupancy

Whether anyone is home, from the indoor motion sensors. An empty house switches the lights off and, in automatic mode, the AC and heater.

📷Camera

CLEAR or an "unauthorised person" notice. The camera records the event in History but does not sound the alarm.

💨Gas Level

The gas reading as a percentage. Above 30% is DANGEROUS and triggers the alarm.

💡Lights & ❄️ AC/Heater

The live state of each appliance, with the reason shown for lights and a Manual tag whenever you are controlling them.

📋Recent Activity & Incidents

The latest events as they happen. Open View All for the full searchable History, which you can also export as CSV.

3Homeowner controls

Each control has the same simple idea: take over manually, or press Resume normal to give it back to the automatic system.

💡

Light

Lights, all rooms
ON

Lights stay on, whatever the brightness or occupancy.

OFF

Lights stay off, even if it is dark.

Resume normal

Automatic again: lights come on when brightness drops below 30% and someone is home, and go off in an empty house.

🚪

Gate

Main gate
OPEN

You are opening the gate yourself. It shows OPEN and is treated as authorised, so it will not trigger a breach alarm while you hold it open.

CLOSE

The gate is closed and the sensor keeps guarding it. Any new movement or opening is still a breach.

Resume normal

Back to fully automatic sensor monitoring.

🚪

Door

Front & back doors
OPEN

Same as the gate: you are opening the doors, so it is authorised and will not raise an alarm.

CLOSE

Doors are closed and still guarded by their sensors.

Resume normal

Back to automatic monitoring.

🛡️

Security Mode

The entire system
ON

The whole system is armed and running: sensors scan, the alarm can trigger and appliances follow their rules.

OFF

Switches the entire system off immediately. Monitoring pauses, any active alarm is cleared, and lights, AC and heater go off. Cards show PAUSED. Use this with care: nothing is protecting the home while it is off.

Resume normal

Starts the system again in its normal armed state. Each sensor reports fresh readings within about 15 seconds.

❄️

AC / Heater

Climate
AC

AC on, heater off, regardless of the weather. Handy when you want cooling no matter what the sensor says.

HEATER

Heater on, AC off, regardless of the weather. For example, when you feel cold on a hot day because you are unwell.

OFF

Both AC and heater off.

Resume normal

The weather decides again: AC when hot, heater when cold, both off when comfortable or when nobody is home.

🔇Silence Siren

Stops the siren sound while the breach is still being monitored. The alarm resets itself once every layer is clear.

📧Send Test Email

Sends a test alert to the homeowner's email so you can confirm that notifications are working.

⚠️Priority order: Security Mode OFF overrides everything. Your manual choices come next, and automatic logic only applies to controls set to Resume normal.

4Alarm & siren

What happens when something goes wrong.

A breach is detected

The gate sensor, door sensors or gas sensor report a problem. The Home Status turns red, the alarm card blinks and the siren sounds with a "pim-pim" tone. An alert email is sent to the homeowner.

Silence the siren if needed

Press Silence Siren. The sound stops immediately, but the breach stays on the dashboard and keeps being monitored.

The alarm resets by itself

When every layer is clear again, the alarm returns to ALL CLEAR and you are notified.

🔊Siren sound: browsers only allow sound after you have tapped or clicked the page once. After that first tap, the siren plays automatically.

7Troubleshooting

Quick answers to common questions.

The dashboard says the worker is OFFLINE

The monitoring worker has stopped or has not reported for 30 seconds. Check that the worker service is running, then wait a few seconds for the heartbeat to return.

I pressed Security Mode OFF and the cards say PAUSED

That is expected: the whole system is off. Press ON or Resume normal on Security Mode to start it again.

I opened the gate but nothing sounded. Is it broken?

No. A gate you open with the control is authorised, so it does not raise an alarm. Press CLOSE or Resume normal when done.

My lights did not change when it got dark

Check whether Light shows a Manual tag. If it does, press Resume normal so the system can decide again.

The siren has no sound

Tap or click anywhere on the page once, because browsers block sound until you interact. Also check that the siren has not been silenced and your device volume is up.

I did not receive the alert email

Press Send Test Email. If it fails, the email service key or recipient address is not set correctly for the deployment.
